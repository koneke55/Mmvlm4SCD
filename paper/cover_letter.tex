\opening{Dear Editor,}

I am pleased to submit the enclosed manuscript, ``\textit{Multimodal
Modeling of Sickle Cell Disease: A Quantitative Study of Severity
Stratification and Survival Prediction},'' for consideration as an
original research article.

Sickle Cell Disease (SCD) is the most common monogenic disorder
worldwide, but its phenotypic course remains poorly predicted by
single-modality scores. Modern care produces clinical, genomic,
imaging and longitudinal information that no released open framework
yet integrates end-to-end. We address that gap with
\textbf{Mmvlm4SCD}, an open multimodal deep-learning framework that
fuses four modality-specific encoders via attention and is trained
jointly with cross-entropy and Cox partial likelihood for severity
stratification and time-to-event survival.

Our submission contributes:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item An open, reproducible architecture with three pluggable
        fusion strategies and a dual-task head.
  \item A literature-calibrated synthetic SCD cohort plus a curated
        registry of 14 public SCD data sources, lowering the barrier
        to multimodal SCD benchmarking.
  \item A quantitative study with seed-replicated metrics, bootstrap
        CIs, fusion comparison, time-dependent Brier / IBS, and
        subgroup analysis. Strong tabular baselines remain
        competitive --- an honest negative result that motivates
        evaluation on credentialed cohorts (dbGaP, MIMIC-IV-SCD,
        UK Biobank, NHLBI CuRe-SCD).
\end{enumerate}

The manuscript follows the \textbf{TRIPOD+AI} reporting guidance
(checklist as Table~S1). All code, configurations, and analysis
artefacts are released under MIT at
\href{https://github.com/koneke55/Mmvlm4SCD}
{github.com/koneke55/Mmvlm4SCD}; every number in the manuscript is
regenerated end-to-end from the released scripts.

This work is original; it has not been published elsewhere and is not
under simultaneous consideration by any other journal. The author has
no competing interests to declare; funding was not required for this
study.
No human or animal data were used; all experiments are computed on a
fully synthetic, parametric cohort generated by the released code, so
ethics approval was not required.

We believe this submission will be of interest to readers working at
the intersection of haematology, multimodal medical AI, survival
analysis, and reproducible clinical-prediction research.

\closing{Sincerely,}
